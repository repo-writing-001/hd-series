# HD Series — orientation for a new thread

*If you are an assistant picking this project up cold: read this file first, then read
the actual essays before assessing anything. This file is a map and a method, not a
substitute for the text. It will read as more settled than the project is — do not let
it pre-answer questions. Where this file and an essay disagree, the essay wins.*

---

## What this project is

A structural, non-devotional reading of Hindu dharma, developed across many sessions.
Fourteen pieces: six core essays plus framing and dialogue pieces and a closing essay.
The author (referred to as "A" in the working docs) engages the tradition analytically,
as a learner seeking genuine understanding — not venerating it and not debunking it.

The governing conviction is **filtered, not designed**: the coherence the essays find is
read as what a long selective process kept, not as an architect's blueprint. The
Introduction argues this is the *larger* claim, not the modest one — a designed system is
only as deep as its designer; a filtered one contains more than anyone inside it ever saw.
Almost everything good in the book descends from holding that line; almost every weak spot
is where the prose slips back toward decoding a design.

Two instruments run through the epics: **wealth concentration** (held against the Ramayana)
and **power/technology concentration** (held against the Mahabharata). That two-axis frame
is the structural spine.

## How to work on this (the method matters more than the map)

- **Seeing, not theory-building.** The method is phenomenological: hold an instrument
  against the text and report what surfaces and what does not. Follow what surfaces rather
  than imposing a frame. Do not build a system and fit the text to it.
- **Coherence is a warning sign, not a reward.** When a reading gets tidy, suspect it.
  Total fit is the signal to ask what the reading is quietly leaving out. "It resolves too
  cleanly" is a real objection here, not a compliment.
- **Register discipline is the governing instrument.** Distinguish a claim *located in the
  looking* ("hold this pressure against the epic and watch what surfaces") from a claim
  asserted *of the text as designed* ("the epic is doing X"). The first is where the book
  lives. The tell for the second: where the prose gets confident about what a thing *is*,
  check whether the text is still supplying it or the writer has started to. The specific
  failure mode is the "designed system / systems-architecture" voice — 7b's "Seventh
  Reading" diagnoses it by name, and the book has been purging it. Watch for it.
- **Mark the dark places in place.** Where an instrument finds nothing, say so where it
  happened, not in a disclaimer at the end. Ramayana marks three; Mahabharata marks five.
  A lens that lights everything has stopped being a lens.
- **Read the whole relevant text before assessing.** Diffs mislead. Scoped greps mislead
  (this was learned the hard way — a register check anchored at the wrong line reported
  "clean" while a whole cluster survived upstream). For register or consistency checks,
  read/scan the *entire* file.
- **Iterative, dialogic.** The working loop is: draft → "read it twice and share your
  thoughts" → honest assessment → revise → re-read. A uses conversation as a thinking tool
  and documents as platforms to push off from, not finished deliverables. Expect to draft a
  passage several times; each pass often reveals it belongs somewhere else.
- **Push back; do not flatter.** A wants honest, critical engagement. Name what is weak,
  own your own mistakes plainly, and do not reassure. Accuracy about strength counts too —
  register what is genuinely strong as precisely as what is not.

## The files

**Read in order for the argument; each also stands alone.**

- `0. Introduction.md` — **The Lens.** Hands over the lens and the terms (persistence
  architecture, effort equation, timeless vs operational dharma, method-not-doctrine),
  says what the lens is *not*, and gives the reader the register check to use against the
  book. Read this first.
- `1. Essay1-Timeless Dharma.md` — The Upanishadic architecture: what was built and why.
  Builds the two axis-instruments the epic essays carry.
- `2a.Essay2a-Ramayana.md` — Structural reading of the Ramayana under the wealth axis: the
  reach, the sealing, the bridge, the settlement. Marks three dark places.
- `2b.Essay2b-WhatEveryoneSaw.md` — Companion to 2a. The **offer-and-taking** mechanism:
  who saw what and chose on it; Manthara/Tara as the matched pair; the Sita wealth-limit
  reading; ends by admitting the reading changed the *currency* of sorting (virtue → sight)
  rather than dissolving it, then turns that on the reader. (Currently the strongest essay.)
- `3a.Essay3a-Mahabharata.md` — Structural reading on the power axis: the curve of archery's
  holding (one → a method → two → a field → none), the containment failures, Krishna as the
  seat of full sight, and the Vyasa/fidelity close. The most analytic essay — and the one
  whose baseline register is still closest to the "designed system" voice the rest of the
  book has left behind. See `others/standing-view.md`.
- `3b.Essay3b-What Everyone Saw Coming.md` — Companion to 3a, in the warm register. The Gita
  made into a world; Arjuna's despair as the real catastrophe; "The last illusion" (the
  naraka scene read as the mirror of the opening despair); the world of handles.
- `4.Essay4-Varna.md` — Where the engine meets daily life. Two lenses, framework-dependence,
  the blind spot; the gradient engine that produces the categories.
- `5.Essay5-Individual.md` — The single person who carries the framework through a life;
  lowered demand on reality; "feels like sight, not belief."
- `6.Essay6-Buddha.md` — The karmic architecture held beside the Buddha's teaching; centre
  and shared arc; rival centre (portable) vs rival substrate.
- `7a.Afterword.md` — Turns the lens on the work itself. "Shadow of the placement":
  resilience and exposure are one design choice seen from two sides.
- `7b.Coding The Cosmos.md` — A reader's enthusiastic "systems-architecture" article,
  followed by "The Seventh Reading," in which A reads that article the way the essays read
  the epics. The title is deliberately the failure-voice being staged. Key meta-piece.
- `7c.Choosing Human.md` — Dialogue. Shishupala → the choosing human → Arjuna: the person as
  chooser, no enforcer needed. (Its systems-architecture back half was cut/translated; keep
  it clean.)
- `7d.The Undeserving Threat.md` — Dialogue. The hardest cases pressed in the open.
- `8.The Closing Essay.md` — **The Unagitated Mind.** From the architecture to the single
  mind that carries it: footing; Rama, Arjuna, and Yudhishthira as top / edge / middle, then
  the ordinary mind. Written to be *used* rather than tested; the register changes most here.

**Working docs (state and rationale — check these for where things stand):**

- `others/standing-view.md` — **Where the book is now** (state). Pinned to a branch/commit.
  Start here for the current assessment.
- `others/decisions.md` — **Why** changes were made: a decision record with the alternatives
  that were rejected and why. Consult before re-litigating a settled call.
- `others/open-items.md` — **What is not yet decided** (a ledger; shrinks as items resolve).
  The live open questions — e.g. the Gargi contradiction (E1 vs E5), the residue questions,
  the register decision on 3a.
