## Decisions

A decision record, appended per round. Not a diary — it keeps the reasoning that survived and the alternatives rejected, i.e. the things a diff can't show and that you'd need in order to *revisit* a call rather than just see it was made. If an entry wouldn't change how someone reconsiders the decision, it doesn't belong here.

## patch2 — the Yudhishthira round

**The core decision: one figure, three registers.**
Yudhishthira was read three ways over the round, and the ways don't fit in one place. Rather than pick one, we split him by register, each piece taking only the part it can hold:

**3b** gets the *argued-over* Yudhishthira — the ending, the dog, the naraka illusion, the mirror with Arjuna. 3b is where seeing is still being done.

**8** gets the *stood-beside* Yudhishthira — the ordinary mind that upheld the worldly sense of doing right under weight. 8 is the essay that rests, so it takes only the standable part, exactly as it already does with Rama.

**7c** keeps *one clause* — Yudhishthira as one instance of partial sight in the list leading to Arjuna. The full reading now lives in 3b, which is what licensed cutting him here to almost nothing.

**Rejected: put the naraka mirror in 8.** Tried it. 8 rests and rests *partly by leaving things out* (it says so of itself). The naraka mirror is analytic — it pulls the reader up into watching the epic operate — and dropping it into 8 un-rested the essay at the exact moment the book is meant to stop arguing. So the reading went to 3b and only the figure went to 8.

**Rejected: rewrite 7c's back half into the right register.** The back half wasn't a style problem to repaint. Once 3b and the Closing carry Arjuna's despair and the Gita in the book's own voice, 7c's back half was revealed as *recap* — the same scenes a third time. The systems-architecture idiom (operating system, hardware reset, software patch, physics of the hack) had disguised the redundancy as content. So the fix was deletion, not translation: ~20 exchanges down to 8. Note this is the idiom 7b's "Seventh Reading" explicitly diagnoses as the lens degrading under enthusiasm — 7c's back half was performing, in the author's mouth, the failure the adjacent piece names.

**8's epithet, set to undercut itself.** "dharma-raja, the one *held to be* the ultimate upholder of dharma" keeps him in the seeing (not "is"), which also immunises the paragraph against the reader who knows he failed — you can't contradict a *perception* with the dice game. "not the summit of dharma but the keeping of the plain thing" turns the grand title into the humble act, and resolves the seam with the following "not carried by Ramas": the named king reads as the middle mind, not the summit. Reseamed that next paragraph ("And it was never only him… the numberless who had neither") so three paragraphs run as a descent — poles → one named mind → the nameless many — instead of striking the same note three times.

**Word choices worth recording (they were argued, not casual).**
8: "worldly" over "ordinary" (too plebeian) and over "moral" (too clinical for 8's register); "upheld" over "held" (a load-bearing verb neutralises the "merely worldly" shadow without a defending clause).
7c: "square you stand on / whole board" kept — it's a *seeing/choosing* metaphor (limits of sight, locality of choice), on the right side of the line;  "machine"/"architecture" removed as *mechanism* metaphors.

**Process learning this round.** The seams told us what was missing. The dog wouldn't sit still in 7c because it belonged to a reading (3b's) that didn't exist yet; once 3b existed, everything downstream resolved. And the full read corrected the diff read: the register discipline was not a new overlay the patch imposed — E4/E5/E6/7a/7b already held it, and the epic essays were being harmonised toward it. The remaining work on this book is register *consistency*, not new content.

## Readability round — 7c / 7d / 8

*(Follows the Yudhishthira round; renumber to the branch's patch convention on merge.)*

**The core decision: separate friction-difficulty from carrying-difficulty.**
A readability pass on a book whose aesthetic is *thinking-in-progress* can do real damage if it treats all difficulty as friction. The round ran on one distinction. Incidental difficulty — overbuilt sentences, stacked negations, abstract-noun chains, a twisted word order — comes out with no loss. *Carrying* difficulty stays: where the hardness is a thematic echo, a deliberate rhythm, or load-bearing vertigo, smoothing it introduces a falsehood. Every call below is an instance of that line.

**The real work was register separation, not readability.** Named in STANDING-VIEW. The highest-value edit of the round was cutting the testing-voice from the Closing, because that voice belongs in 7d and was contaminating the one piece meant to rest. The sentence work was secondary.

**8 — cut the register self-declaration.** The "a word about what this piece is … written for a reader to use rather than to test" paragraph is gone. *Rejected: keep a short "to-be-used" beat.* The italic preamble ("set down once, in a single voice … and for you") and the tail ("the pieces before this one press the harder parts … this is not the hour for that") already carry the signal; a full self-justifying paragraph in the resting piece is the testing-voice announcing itself. Same logic as patch2's "rejected: naraka mirror in 8" — 8 rests partly by leaving things out, and self-narration un-rests it.

**8 — collapsed the self-auditing tail.** The "this is the part that comes to rest" and "choosing which hour a thing belongs to … it is worth saying I have made it" paragraphs went to one reconciling line ("The Rama here is only the part of him you can stand beside …"). The "I have made a choice here" watching is the essay auditing its own edit at the moment it should simply be steady.

**8 — restored "the ground with it."** The two-axis rewrite briefly cut it ("the horizon falls away, and it is what Krishna gives back"). *Rejected: leave it cut.* The essay's master image is footing/ground; the phrase ties the horizon-loss to the ground-loss at the passage that most needs to reach the close ("remember how to stand"). Costs nothing to keep, re-links the spine.

**8 — two-axis passage rewritten linear.** The original made the reader hold four states at once (horizon-alone, demand-alone, both, and each failing the opposite way). Rewritten one-idea-per-sentence. Kept "horizon" and "the demand of the present"; standardised the short handle to "the moment" (the paired term had drifted between "present" and "moment"). Kept "worldly," "upheld," "held to be" per patch2.

**7d — removed the systems/management idiom (register calls, not just readability).** executive → ruler; two "geopolitical"s cut; "actionable" → "something she can act on"; "diagnostic manual / distributed architecture / apex actors" → "a set of cases … how an order like this comes through even its greatest actors." Recorded as register decisions: this is the 7b "Seventh Reading" failure-voice in the author's mouth, exactly as patch2 found in 7c's back half. What made it easy: the readability lens and the register lens picked the *same words* — the jargon was both the hard-to-read and the design-voice.

**7d — left the "am I a defence" section hard.** *Rejected: smooth it.* The vertigo — A auditing where he folds vs holds, then admitting he *relocated* a finding rather than conceding it — is the content; the reader is meant to be made to distrust. Only the sentence lengths were cut, so the reader fights the idea and not the syntax.

**7d — restored one compressed "renewable, deniable, fastening" fragment.** The pass had unpacked both staccato fragments into plain clauses. *Rejected: keep both unpacked.* The compression is a deliberate device; flattening it twice in one section reads least like A. Kept one compressed (the first fire passage) and one plain (the later "strips the personal claim out" passage) — which tracks the original's own variation (the first was always a fragment, the second a fuller sentence).

**7c — softened the Vishnu/deva signpost.** The clarified form first read "Vishnu belongs there [with the ground of being], not among the devas." *Rejected: that positive placement.* It over-commits Vishnu = Brahman, which cuts against E1's saguna/nirguna care (Vishnu as a saguna form, a bridge, held distinct from the nirguna ground). Softened to "stands on that side of the line, not among the devas" — keeps the clarity (the reader can see *why* the ground of being is a separate category) without the commitment.

**Corrections worth recording.** 7d: unified the writ's proper noun to *Ikshvaku* — A's line had drifted to "Kosala's writ" while R's said "Ikshvaku writ," for the same thing; Ikshvaku also matches Rama's stated basis ("the land is Ikshvaku land"). Restored *paramount power* over the plainer "great power" — paramountcy is the precise concept in dispute: a paramount power's writ over subordinate polities, assumed and untested until it costs something.

**Process learning this round.** The zero net cut on 7c/7d is the evidence their density was carrying something; a pass that cut them to length would have been cutting the seeing. And the caution to carry forward: the pass that suited the Closing is calibrated for a piece meant to *rest*. Run the same instinct at full strength over the six analytic essays and it will read the thinking-in-progress texture — the provisional hedges, the author noticing mid-sentence — as excess and sand it off, which is the one thing those essays can't lose. Transferable rule: **cut meta and plain the single hardest node in a piece meant to rest; leave both alone in a piece meant to test.**
