## What changed this round (the readability round)

A readability-and-register pass over the three framing/closing pieces the prose worked hardest in: **7c 1.20**, **7d 0.24**, **8 0.45**. Versions unchanged — bump on merge.

The word-counts name the shape of it: 7c and 7d lost *zero* net words, the Closing lost ~280. That is the finding, not an accident of counting. Only one piece carried real excess, and the excess was of one kind — the author narrating his own restraint. 7c and 7d had almost no *incidental* difficulty to remove; their density was load-bearing, and the near-zero net cut is the evidence the pass recognised it.

So the round's real work was **register separation, not sentence-smoothing.** The three pieces now hold three distinct voices cleanly: 7c is patient joint-inquiry (two people building a floor), 7d is adversarial self-audit (the writer cross-examined, cornering himself), 8 is resting statement (one voice setting one thing down). Before the pass, 8 was leaking the 7d voice — the "I have made a choice here," the "this is the part that comes to rest" — the testing-register bleeding into the one piece meant to be *used*, not tested. The single most consequential edit of the round was pulling that voice back out of 8. The sentence-level work was real but secondary to it. Calls in DECISIONS.md.

Per piece. **8** — register self-declaration paragraph cut; the self-auditing tail collapsed to one reconciling line; the two-axis passage rewritten linear (one idea per sentence; "horizon" and "the demand of the moment" kept, short handle standardised on "moment"); "the ground with it" restored so the passage lands back on the essay's footing image. The Closing now rests where it used to narrate resting. **7d** — Vali/fire syntax relieved; the pativrata passage re-led with the human fact so its payoff lands on contact; the systems/management idiom removed (executive → ruler; two "geopolitical"s cut; "actionable" plained; "diagnostic manual / distributed architecture / apex actors" → "a set of cases … how an order like this comes through even its greatest actors"); the "am I a defence" section's difficulty left intact — only its sentences shortened, because the vertigo is the content; one compressed "renewable, deniable, fastening" fragment restored (one compressed, one plain). **7c** — lightest of the three; count-negation redundancy trimmed; the Vishnu/deva signpost clarified then softened to "stands on that side of the line, not among the devas," kept out of asserting Vishnu *is* Brahman per E1's saguna/nirguna care.

One convergence worth pinning: in 7d the readability lens and the register-discipline lens landed on the *same words*. The jargon that read as abstract was the design-voice — the 7b "Seventh Reading" failure, in the author's mouth again, exactly as patch2 found in 7c's back half. Where the prose is hardest to read, check first whether it is the design-voice; twice now it has been.

## What the whole-book read established (still holds)

One sustained argument, one register, one governing conviction: *filtered, not designed.* The strongest pieces turn the method on the method itself (7b's "Seventh Reading"; the Afterword's shadow-of-the-placement; 2b's admission that the sight-reading changed the *currency* of sorting, not dissolved it). The disciplined register is not a patch-era overlay — E4/E5/E6/7a/7b already held it; the epic essays and framing pieces are being harmonised toward it. The book's remaining work is register *consistency* and the open content residues, not new content. Strongest: 7b, the Afterword, 2b, E5, E6, 3b. These are original and hold.
